Steps to Start Your Business in South Africa

1

Choose a Unique Business Name

Start by thinking carefully about your business name. Your name should represent what your business does and should be distinguishable from existing registered businesses.

Before committing to a name, check whether it is available for registration.

2

Register Your Business

In South Africa, companies can be registered through the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC).

You can learn more about registration through:

  • CIPC: www.cipc.co.za
  • BizPortal: www.bizportal.gov.za

Make sure you understand the type of business structure you are registering and the requirements that apply to you.

3

Get Your Business Documents in Order

Keep important documents relating to your business organised and safely stored.

  • Registration documents
  • Identification documents
  • Business address information
  • Tax-related documents where applicable
  • Contracts and agreements
4

Open a Business Bank Account

Once your business is registered, investigate business banking options offered by South African banks.

Compare fees, transaction limits, online banking, payment facilities and other services before choosing an account.

5

Understand Your Tax Responsibilities

Depending on your business structure, income and activities, you may have tax obligations.

Learn about your responsibilities and make sure your business remains compliant with the South African Revenue Service (SARS).

Keep proper financial records from the beginning. Good records can save you a lot of trouble later.

6

Understand Licences and Permits

Not every business needs a special licence, but some industries and activities may have additional requirements.

Check whether your type of business requires permits, licences, certificates or approval from a municipality or another authority.

7

Create a Simple Business Plan

You don't need a 100-page document to start thinking like an entrepreneur.

Write down:

  • What your business sells
  • Who your customers are
  • What problem you solve
  • How much it will cost to operate
  • How much you intend to charge
  • How customers will find you
8

Calculate Your Costs

Before spending money, understand what your business actually needs.

  • Equipment
  • Stock
  • Transport
  • Marketing
  • Website and software
  • Rent or workspace
  • Professional services

Start as lean as possible and avoid unnecessary expenses.

9

Build Your Brand

Give people a reason to remember your business.

Think about your logo, colours, business message, social media presence and the way you communicate with customers.

10

Launch a Website — If You Need One

A website can help customers understand your business, find your contact details and learn about your products or services.

But don't let the lack of a website stop you from starting. You can begin small and improve your online presence as your business grows.

11

Start Marketing

Tell people that your business exists.

Depending on your business, you can use social media, WhatsApp, community groups, referrals, networking, flyers, events and other marketing channels.

12

Start — Even If It Is Small

Your first version doesn't have to be perfect.

Start with what you have, learn from your customers, improve your service and keep moving forward.

The goal is not to look successful. The goal is to build something that can eventually become successful.

⚠️ Important

This page is intended as a simple starting guide and not as legal, accounting or financial advice. Business registration, tax, licensing and other requirements can depend on your circumstances and industry. Always verify current requirements with the relevant South African authorities before making important decisions.
